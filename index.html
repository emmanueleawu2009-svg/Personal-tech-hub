FAQ

A few things made clear.

Are the listed prices fixed?

No. They are starter prices. Larger scopes, extra pages/slides or additional revisions can be quoted separately.

Do I have to pay immediately?

No. You can send a project request first. Payment is an optional next step when you are ready to start.

What payment method is supported?

The website is prepared for Paystack. The live payment flow uses server-side transaction initialization and verification, while your secret key stays on the server.

Can you work with my existing file?

Yes. Presentation makeovers and document cleanup are specifically designed for projects where you already have content.
